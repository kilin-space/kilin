# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Everyone taking part in Kilin — issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews — is expected to
treat others with respect. In practice that means:

- assume good faith, and give feedback on the work rather than the person;
- accept that reviewers and maintainers may decline a change, and say why;
- keep discussion technical and on topic; and
- respect that contributors work across time zones, languages, and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or sexualized language,
publishing someone's private information without permission, and sustained disruption of
discussion.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior by [emailing the maintainer](mailto:qiyijiazhen@gmail.com). Reports
are handled privately.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying this standard and may edit, remove, or reject
contributions and comments that do not follow it, and may temporarily or permanently ban any
contributor for behavior they judge harmful.
